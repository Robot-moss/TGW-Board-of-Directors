### Fact 1 — 2024: the fund held ~47% equities and captured a fifth of the equity market's return

The NGX returned **+37.65%**. The Balanced Fund returned **+7.91%**.

The mechanism: the CBN took the MPR from 18.75% to 27.50% inside twelve months. That is the worst outcome a balanced fund can produce: **full equity volatility, almost none of the equity return.**

## 4. YOUR PENALTY FIGURES DO NOT MATCH THE FACTSHEETS — AND THE REAL DIFFERENCE IS BIGGER

You reported a 2% early-withdrawal penalty on Balanced and 0.01% on the MMF. **Every Coronation factsheet from March 2022 to January 2026 says 1.5% for both.** The difference is not the rate. It is the **base**:

| | Money Market Fund | Balanced Fund |
|---|---|---|
| Early redemption charge | 1.5% **of income earned** | 1.5% **of net proceeds** |
| On ₦5m redeemed after 1 month | ~1.5% of ~₦83,000 = **~₦1,250** | n/a |
| On ₦5m redeemed early | — | 1.5% of ₦5,000,000+ = **~₦75,000+** |
| Minimum holding period | **1 month** | **6 months** |
| Management fee | 1.50% p.a. | 1.50% p.a. |
| Minimum initial / additional | ₦10,000 / ₦5,000 | ₦10,000 / ₦5,000 |
| Risk-reward profile | **2 of 7** | **5 of 7** |

**The Balanced Fund's exit charge is roughly sixty times more punitive in capital terms** — because it is levied on everything you take out, not on what you earned. Your "0.01%" was almost certainly the MMF charge expressed against capital, which is the correct way to read it and explains where the number came from.

**Three further disclosures:**

1. **The MMF's management fee rose from 1.0% to 1.5% between November 2024 and November 2025** — a 50% increase — while the fund grew from ₦8.16bn to ₦61.6bn. Scale went up 8x; no fee benefit was passed to holders. Worth raising with your relationship manager.
2. **Neither fund publishes a Total Expense Ratio.** Anywhere. The 1.5% management fee is a floor, not the all-in cost. **Request the audited TER from Coronation in writing before you allocate.**
3. **Do not trust NairaCompare or KudiCompass for this fund's terms.** NairaCompare lists the MMF's launch date as "March 3, 1958" and fund size as ₦500m (actual: ₦64–104bn). Both sites contradict the factsheet on holding periods and penalties. The factsheet is the authority.

**A caution on the MMF's apparent stability.** It never shows a negative month because it is valued at **amortised cost**, not marked to market. That is accounting, not skill. Your income from it can halve without the capital ever appearing to move.

**That risk is live right now.** The CBN has begun easing — MPR 27.50% → 27.00% (Sep 2025) → 26.50% (Feb 2026, held through July). 91-day T-bills are at 16.30%. The MMF's yield fell from 23.08% in January 2025 to 16.65% in January 2026. **Model this at 17–20%, not 21.79%.**
