## 0. HEADLINE: THE RULE CHANGE THAT REWRITES THIS PLAN

On **28 August 2026** Thailand's Department of Consular Affairs announced changes effective **31 August 2026**. The e-Visa portal was taken offline on 30 August and reopened under new rules.

| # | Change | Effect on David |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Must apply at the Thai mission covering **country of citizenship or permanent/legal residence**. Third-country applications ended. | **Must apply in Nigeria (Abuja).** Vientiane / Ho Chi Minh / Kuala Lumpur / London routes are closed to him unless he holds legal residence there. |
| 2 | **Proof of permanent residence** replaces proof of current location | Nigerian residence proof needed; definition left undefined by MFA → embassy divergence |
| 3 | **Police clearance certificate mandatory for all DTV applicants** | New document, on top of Nigeria's existing NDLEA certificate |
| 4 | Thai language courses removed as a soft power activity | Closes the cheapest soft power option |

The announcement **did not define** what qualifies as proof of permanent residence, producing open inconsistency between missions (Vientiane: nationals/PRs of Laos only; Los Angeles: a driving licence or valid US visa suffices; Moscow: contradicts itself between its English and Russian pages).

**Strategic consequence:** the single biggest lever David had — picking a lenient embassy — is gone. His application now stands or falls at Abuja.

## 1. THE TWO QUALIFYING ROUTES COMPARED

| Dimension | (a) Workcation / Remote Work | (b) Thai Soft Power |
|---|---|---|
| Official document trigger | "Employment contract or employment certificate in their country **or professional portfolio** showcasing digital nomad, remote worker, foreign talent or freelancer status" | "Letter of acceptance from institute or company organizing activities, letter of appointment from hospital/medical centre" |
| Fit for a self-employed retail trader | **Weak.** No employer, no payslips, no client contracts. Several embassies additionally demand 6 months of salary slips and an embassy-authenticated company registration. | **Strong.** Work status is irrelevant; only funds + enrolment matter. |
| Burden sits with | The applicant (must manufacture a credible work paper trail) | The Thai provider (school/gym/hospital supplies the pack) |
| Reported 2025–26 direction | Tightening hard against self-employed | Tightening on course *duration*, not on applicant status |
| Known failure mode | "Self-employed without clear income" being rejected | Course too short (<6 months) → METV issued instead of DTV |
| **Verdict for David** | Second choice at best | **PRIMARY ROUTE** |

### Why workcation is the wrong door for a prop-firm trader

- **ask.in.th, stock traders:** "If you are just a retail platform trader I doubt it." Thai immigration has published no criteria for individual traders.
- **ask.in.th, forex/share trading income:** "That will likely be tricky unless you have a registered company."
- **petchnumnoi.com (Aug 2026):** applicants "without actual jobs (or who apply as self-employed without clear income) are being rejected, whereas those with employment have smoother approvals."
- **Thailand Starter Kit / ExpatDen (2026):** salaried remote workers approved at higher rates than self-employed. Vague "I work online" letters are rejected.
- **ASEAN NOW, member `rkh_`:** advises *avoiding* the workcation pathway entirely in favour of soft power.

**On the specific instruments David holds:**
- A **prop-firm funded-account agreement** is untested in any source found. It is not an employment contract — it is an evaluation/profit-split agreement that typically disclaims an employment relationship. **Unproven, not proof.**
- **Trading own capital** is the weakest case: no counterparty, no invoices, no client.
- A **registered company** with its own registration certificate, corporate tax filing and cover letter is the single highest-value upgrade to a workcation case.
- A **portfolio** works where it shows a *public, multi-year body of work*. The one documented portfolio-only approval (London, 2024) was a vlogger with a 4–5 year website of videos, podcasts and articles. **A brokerage equity curve is not that.**

|---|---|
| **Thai cooking** | School enrolment letter with dates; school business licence; payment receipt. **School packages all of it.** |

**Lowest documentary burden:** terms.law (Dec 2025) rates **medical treatment** simplest, "High" approval. In practice **Thai cooking is lowest-effort for the applicant** because reputable schools assemble and warrant the whole pack. **Muay Thai** carries the heaviest provider stack but is best understood by officers.

**COURSE DURATION IS THE REAL GATE:**

| Duration | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Under 3 months | ~99% rejection |
| 6 months | "Bare minimum"; several embassies now issue a multiple-entry *tourist* visa instead |
| **9–12 months** | **Current safe standard** |
| South Asia / Middle East posts | Assume 12 months minimum (Dhaka demands 1 year) |

Worked example: **Arun Thai Cooking** — 9-month minimum, ≥1 class/week (45–60 classes), **THB 48,900** for 9 months / THB 58,900 for 12 months, plus optional THB 9,500 visa-support package.

## 2. DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS

### Common to both routes (post-31 Aug 2026)
1. Passport, valid 6+ months, blank pages
2. Recent photograph (within 6 months; size varies by post)
3. Completed online application via **thaievisa.go.th** (Abuja accepts no physical applications)
4. Passport biodata copy
5. **Proof of permanent residence** in the country of application *(new)*
6. **Police clearance certificate** *(new)* — recency varies: London 6 months; Washington DC 3 months
7. **Bank statements, last 3 months, ending balance ≥ THB 500,000 each month**
8. Proof of accommodation in Thailand (some posts)
9. English or Thai translations, notarised/legalised
10. **Nigerian nationals: NDLEA Clearance Certificate** (see §5)

### Route (a) Workcation — additional
- Employment contract or certificate from a foreign employer, **or** professional portfolio
- *Where demanded:* 6 months of salary slips / monthly income proof
- *Where demanded:* company registration, embassy-authenticated
- *Recommended:* 1–2 years of tax returns; client invoices reconciling to bank deposits; a specific cover letter

### Route (b) Soft Power — additional
- Letter of acceptance stating course dates and duration (target 9–12 months)
- Provider's business registration / operating licence
- Payment receipt
- Medical: hospital letter of appointment or treatment plan
- Muay Thai: the extended provider stack above

## 3. THE THB 500,000 FINANCIAL REQUIREMENT

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Current figure | **THB 500,000** — unchanged through the Aug 2026 tightening |
| Local equivalents | USD 16,000 (Washington), SGD 20,000 (Singapore), MYR 65,000 (KL), EGP 850,000 (Cairo) |
| Seasoning | **3 months minimum** — "ending balance of no less than 500,000 THB **for each month**". 6 months preferred at stricter posts |
| Whose name | The applicant's own |
| Joint account | Accepted at some posts if both names appear. Moscow expressly allows a "family joint statement with proof of relationship" |
| Currency | THB or foreign-currency equivalent with clear documentation |
| Account type | **Savings / current / withdrawable fixed deposit ONLY.** REJECTED: crypto, stocks, ETFs, brokerage/investment portfolios, pensions, property valuations, credit limits |
| Must it remain? | Not formally required at approval, **but** immigration can request proof on entry and requires updated proof at the 180-day extension. **Treat as permanently committed.** |
| Same 500k for principal + dependant? | **No — assume each person needs their own.** Singapore requires the dependant's own 3-month statement. Some posts accept a family statement at ~THB 1,000,000 total |

**THE DOMINANT REJECTION CAUSE IS THIS LINE ITEM.** One operator attributes ~70% of refusals to bank-statement problems: "parked" money arriving shortly before filing, thin transaction history, wrong account type, or statements dated more than 30 days before submission.

**For David, two risks compound:** (i) funds held in brokerage/prop accounts **do not count** and must move to a bank well in advance; (ii) a trader's account shows volatile flows, so the statement must read as a coherent income story, not as unexplained large transfers.

## 4. THE SPOUSE / DEPENDANT ROUTE (critical for Sarah)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who qualifies | **Legal spouse** and unmarried children under 20 |
| Marriage certificate | **MANDATORY.** Translated and legalised. For Nigerian documents: certification by the Nigerian MFA, then legalisation by the Royal Thai Embassy Abuja. Missing legalisation is a commonly cited rejection cause. |
| **Unmarried partners** | **NO PROVISION. EXPLICITLY INELIGIBLE.** Girlfriends/boyfriends are not next of kin under DTV rules. **Sarah cannot be a dependant until they are legally married.** |
| Timing | **AFTER, not with.** The dependant's file must include the principal's *approved* DTV. |
| Fee | Full visa fee each — THB 10,000 or local equivalent |
| Dependant's financial proof | Own 3-month THB 500,000 statement at Singapore; family/joint statement at Moscow; sponsorship letter required at several posts |
| Sponsor letter | Required, with handwritten signature |
| Police clearance | Required of dependants too |
| Can a dependant work remotely? | **CONFLICTING.** One FAQ says yes (non-Thai employers only); another says no work rights. No official text. **UNRESOLVED.** |
| If the principal's DTV is cancelled? | **NO SOURCE FOUND, official or otherwise. UNRESOLVED.** |

### The strategic read on Sarah — three options, and the obvious one is not the dependant route

1. **Marry, then apply as dependant.** Requires marriage before filing, legalised certificate, David approved first, her own police clearance + NDLEA, plausibly her own THB 500,000, and a second fee. Slow, sequential, and leaves her status derivative of his — with the consequences of his cancellation unknown.
2. **Sarah applies independently under workcation.** Her freelance copywriting in USD produces exactly the evidence the workcation category asks for and David's trading cannot: **named clients, signed contracts, dated invoices, and bank deposits that reconcile to those invoices. Her case is structurally stronger than his.**
3. **Sarah applies independently under soft power.** Same route as David, independent of marital status and of his outcome.

Two independent DTVs cost THB 20,000 and two × THB 500,000 in seasoned funds, but they run **in parallel rather than sequentially, and neither collapses if the other fails.** Given the unknown around dependant status on cancellation, independence is the lower-risk structure.

## 5. NIGERIAN-APPLICANT SPECIFICS

### Which mission
**Royal Thai Embassy Abuja** — 34 Rhine Street, Maitama. Tel (+234) 706 308 0501. **consular.abj@mfa.go.th**

Jurisdiction: Nigeria, Benin, Cameroon, Chad, Republic of the Congo, CAR, Equatorial Guinea, Ghana, São Tomé and Príncipe. **No Thai consulate in Lagos in any official source.**

Post-31 August 2026, **Abuja is the only mission open to a Nigeria-resident Nigerian citizen.** And note: even where a Nigerian holds third-country residence, **the NDLEA requirement follows the passport** — Kuala Lumpur, London and Doha all impose it on Nigerian citizens regardless of where they file.

### Mechanics
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Channel | Online only, **thaievisa.go.th**. Abuja "does not accept physical application" |
| Processing | **10 working days**, excluding submission day |
| When to file | 4–6 weeks before travel; **do not** file more than 3 months before departure |
| **Payment** | **NAIRA DEBIT CARDS WILL NOT PROCESS.** An international Visa or Mastercard is required — resolve before filing |
| DTV fee | THB 10,000 base. The naira/USD figure at Abuja is unverified |

### NDLEA Clearance Certificate — Nigeria-only requirement
Required of Nigerian nationals for Thai visas generally; imposed by Abuja, Kuala Lumpur, London and Doha alike.
- Abuja's checklist: issued **not more than one year** before application
- Filing from outside Nigeria additionally requires Nigerian MFA certification + Royal Thai Embassy Abuja legalisation
- Apply at **drugandvisa.ndlea.gov.ng** · Fee **₦21,950** · Processing **3–7 working days**
- Needs: passport pages 1–6, NIN slip, state of origin certificate, typed signed application letter, **drug integrity test**, sponsor and guarantor documents. **The guarantor must reside in the applicant's state and cannot be a spouse or immediate family member.**

### The new police clearance is a SECOND, separate document
The 31 August 2026 requirement is a criminal-record certificate — distinct from the NDLEA drug clearance. In Nigeria that is the Nigeria Police Force character certificate. **Exactly what Abuja accepts, and its recency, is unverified.** London requires issuance within 6 months, Washington DC within 3 — the window is tight and cannot be rushed at the counter.

### Scrutiny
- Abuja's general visa regulations require applications **in person** for some categories and reference originals produced **at interview** — interviews are a live possibility
- Heightened passport-based scrutiny is reported for Israeli, Russian, Indian, Pakistani and Myanmar holders. **Nigeria is not named in any source found** — but the NDLEA regime is itself a nationality-specific burden, and the practical read is that a Nigerian file will be read closely

## 7. COULD NOT VERIFY — item 1 is a go/no-go

1. **WHETHER THE ROYAL THAI EMBASSY ABUJA PROCESSES DTV APPLICATIONS AT ALL.** The DTV does **not appear** in Abuja's published visa regulations PDF or its document checklist form — both enumerate only Tourist (TR), Non-B, Non-ED, Non-O, Medical (MT), Transit (TS) and Diplomatic. No Abuja DTV page exists; attempts returned 404. Cairo — another African mission — *does* publish a DTV page, so there is no regional bar in principle. One third-party aggregator lists DTV as available to Nigerians (weak evidence). **Since 31 August 2026 there is no alternative mission. If Abuja does not issue DTV, the route is closed.** Resolve by emailing consular.abj@mfa.go.th before spending anything.
2. **What police clearance Abuja accepts, and its required recency** — whether the NPF character certificate suffices, and whether NDLEA satisfies it or is additional.
3. **Whether a DTV dependant may work remotely.** Sources contradict; no official text found.
4. **What happens to a dependant's DTV if the principal's is cancelled.** No source addresses it.
5. **The DTV fee as charged at Abuja** in naira or USD.
6. **Whether a prop-firm funded-account agreement has ever been accepted** as workcation evidence. No reported case either way.
7. **Whether Abuja requires an in-person interview for DTV** specifically.
8. **The authoritative soft power activity list.** Missions publish materially different lists (Cairo: Muay Thai only; Singapore/Moscow/Washington: three; Phnom Penh: six).
9. **Whether Thai language courses were excluded in 2025 or 2026.** The exclusion is the more recent and more consistent claim.
10. **Any announced plan to amend or withdraw the DTV.** Nothing found. The Royal Gazette basis (effective 15 July 2024) remains in force; 2026 activity is documentary tightening, not withdrawal. *Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.*
11. **Current approval/rejection rates.** One operator publishes a 62–94% range by embassy; uncited and unverifiable, and those posts are closed to him anyway. **No data exists for Abuja.**
