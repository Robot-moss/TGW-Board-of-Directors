## 2. TAX - THE DECISIVE VARIABLE

**Thailand:** 180+ days = tax resident. Foreign income taxed on REMITTANCE basis. Post-1 Jan
2024 income taxable if resident in the year derived AND remitted. Progressive PIT to 35%.
As of 7 Sep 2026 the remittance basis still stands; worldwide-taxation proposals NOT enacted.
KEY: only money brought into Thailand is taxed. Capital kept offshore is untouched.

**UAE:** 0% personal income tax. No remittance rules, no CGT on individuals. 9% corporate
only above AED 375,000 (~$102,000) profit. No policy ambiguity.
