**4e. Rule 1 v2, ratified 1 October 2026** — Clause 1 Odion floor (absolute) · Clause 2 default to MMF · Clause 3 Channel A, 20% of annual surplus, clock-neutral · Clause 4 Channel B, uncapped suspension with a soft trigger at ₦941,666, clock-stretching · Clause 5 review.
