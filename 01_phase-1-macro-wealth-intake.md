## 4. ESTATE & TITLE - CRITICAL DEFECT

Both inherited apartments derive from the late father's estate. Father died INTESTATE
January 2021. Letters of Administration HAVE been obtained, in the names of the subject,
two siblings and an uncle as guardian. Four 2-bed units were divided among four siblings
by a written sibling agreement (lawyer-drafted, witnessed) in 2023.

**A sibling agreement is not a legal transfer of title.** Required chain: Letters of
Administration (obtained) -> Deed of Assignment / Deed of Family Partition -> stamping
(1.5% stamp duty, FIRS) -> Governor's / Minister's Consent under the Land Use Act ->
registration at the land registry. Timeline 3-12 months; perfection costs commonly add
10-20% of value.

**Consequences while unperfected:** cannot be sold cleanly; cannot be used as collateral;
will not satisfy immigration proof-of-funds; the claim is contestable if a sibling dies;
an unperfected interest loses to a registered interest in court.

deliberate joint family decision taken in 2021 when the circumstances of the father's death
